How to Play

You are the ADAFCO, operating from the C2 Building TOC, which is part of the DAL near the island’s center. Your mission is to execute a 360° Point-Defense operation: prevent fratricide, manage track engagement, and prosecute hostile air threats as directed by the SL. Success requires speed, judgment, and decisive task prioritization.

Mission Flow

  1. For best training value: All internal and exteranl calls should be simulated before pressing each corresponding button.
  2. COVER Phase
    The Senior Director (SL) calls: “COVER Track 12345, B/E 123/45.”
    • Left-click the track.
    • Evaluate geometry & ID, then press A (AFFIRM) or N (NEGATE).
  3. AFFIRM → ENGAGE
    When the track closes, SL will order ENGAGE automatically or after your AFFIRM.
    When tagged ENGAGE, press W (AWAY) to fire. Two missiles are launched from the nearest in-geometry unit.
  4. VANISH / HOLD FIRE
    Hits remove the track automatically. To abort an intercept, press H (HOLD FIRE) with the engaged track selected.

Top Bar Buttons

  • TAGS – cycle scope overlays (OFF → B/E → JTN → INFO → ALL → ALT)
  • Compass – overlay fixed 8-point compass rings
  • Map – toggle sat/outline background
  • Help – you’re here

Action Bar (bottom-right)

  • REDWOOD – arms “weapons-hot” in a quadrant
    • Tap REDWOOD → choose NW NE SW SE.
    • Hot for 5 min or until friendlies enter.
    • Suppresses ROE penalties in that sector.
  • FIREBALL – global TBM defense switch
    Arms Patriots (50 NM gate) and NASAMS (20 NM gate) for all incoming TBM waves.

Scoring

  • +1 Valid missile kill
  • +2 FIREBALL intercept
  • –1 Birds/shots trashed (miss if no geometry)
  • –5 ROE violation (engaged without authority)
  • –6 Friendly fire (miss)
  • –20 Fratricide
  • –5 TBM craters island
  • –7 ABT damages an asset

Island-wide search radar. Can detect out to ~200 NM (depending on track altitude). May be damaged by bombs/cruise missiles (10 min downtime).

Threat Types

  • Hostile aircraft – maneuver, drop precision bombs at 10 NM & 5 NM. Heavy groups start at Level 3+
  • TBM waves – groups of 1 to 3 ballistic missiles start at Level 3+. Use FIREBALL to prime auto-engage.
  • Cruise missiles – low-alt clustered shots start at Level 2+.
  • Friendlies – Returning to the Island on Procedural MRR. Descend from 20 NM / 200 MSL; IDENT, Do not engage!

Levels & Progression

  1. Level 1 – 10 hostiles.
  2. Level 2 – +5 hostiles, adds cruise-missile raid.
  3. Level 3 – TBM wave ‎(+ FIREBALL).
  4. Level 4+ – Random radar outages.
