Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us.

Introduction

Welcome to Intuition Trades. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website.

Information We Collect

Intuition Trades does not store any personal user data on our own servers. All authentication and account data is securely managed by Google (via Firebase Authentication), and all payment information is handled exclusively by Stripe.

  • Authentication: We use Google and Firebase Authentication to manage sign-ins. Your account credentials are never stored by us.
  • Payments: All billing and subscription data is managed through Stripe, a PCI-compliant provider. We do not process or store any credit card information directly.
  • Session Data: We may use basic session data (e.g., login state or role type) in your browser to personalize your experience.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use limited cookies to maintain your login session and track simple usage patterns. These do not contain sensitive personal data and are not used for external advertising.

Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted services:

  • Google Firebase: for authentication and database functionality.
  • Stripe: for subscription payments and billing management.

These providers are fully responsible for the storage and protection of your account and billing data.

Your Rights

You may request access to or deletion of your data at any time. However, as we do not store any sensitive information directly, such requests should be directed to Google or Stripe if it pertains to authentication or payments, respectively.

Contact Us

If you have any concerns about your privacy, feel free to reach out to us at admin@intuitiontrades.com.
