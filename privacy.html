Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us.

Introduction

Welcome to Intuition Trades. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website.

Information We Collect

Intuition Trades is moving to a public, account-free experience. We do not use login accounts, subscription billing, or payment processing as part of the active product.

  • Public Usage: You can browse and use public tools without creating an account.
  • Operational Data: We may collect basic request and page-use telemetry needed to keep the site reliable and improve the product.
  • Browser Storage: We may use limited local browser storage for preferences such as dismissing guidance messages.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use limited browser storage and basic analytics to understand product usage and keep the experience functional. These are not used to run a login or billing system.

Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted services:

  • Vercel: for hosting, serverless APIs, and operational logging.
  • Market Data Providers: for stock and market information displayed by public tools.

These providers process only the operational data needed to deliver the public site and its tool outputs.

Your Rights

You may contact us to ask questions about operational data we control. Because the active product no longer uses account login or subscription billing, there is no account profile or payment record to manage through this site.

Contact Us

If you have any concerns about your privacy, feel free to reach out to us at admin@intuitiontrades.com.
