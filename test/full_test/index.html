Manual Batch Analysis

This tool fetches Monte Carlo, Technical, Sentiment, Insider, Fundamental, and Congress trading for each ticker. By default, the aggregator is the average of all six (0..100), but with market-condition-specific weights & scaling (Bear/Bull/Neutral). If you check “Use Manual Weights & Scaling,” you can override them.
