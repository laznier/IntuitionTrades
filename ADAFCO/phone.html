Free mode — Ctrl+R sets Bullseye (Ctrl-click a static)

How to Play

Practice runs up to 10 Cover→Engage→Away→Kill sequences:

  1. Cover: Click the prompted track + A (Affirm). Press N (Negate) to stay until A.
  2. Engage: Click + W (Away).
  3. Kill: Click + K. Auto-kill in 10 s.

1 point per correct sequence; after 10 rounds, see GAME OVER + score.

Hotkeys

  • C: COVER (free mode)
  • A: AFFIRM
  • N: NEGATE
  • W: AWAY
  • E: ENGAGE
  • K: KILL
