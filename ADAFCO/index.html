How to Play

You are the ADAFCO, operating from the TOC co-located with the DAL at the island’s center. Your mission is to execute a 360° Point-Defense operation: prevent fratricide, manage track engagement, and prosecute hostile air threats as directed by the SL. Success requires speed, judgment, and decisive task prioritization.

Mission Flow

  1. COVER
    The SL will order: “COVER Track 12345, B/E 123/45.”
    Primary select (left-click) the correct track, assess ID, flight profile, and probability of kill (PK), then acknowledge with:
    “Copy Track 12345.”
  2. ICC/TD Coordination
    Relay the COVER command to the ICC/TD via simulated radio:
    “GUARDIAN, BIGFOOT, Cover Group B/E 123/45, 27k, Track South, Hostile, NW Quadrant.”
    • If PK is high (inside Patriot wedge or NASAM ring), press A (Affirm).
    • If no solution exists, press N (Negate). You may update to A later if conditions change.
  3. Engagement
    When the track is within range:
    • TX: “GUARDIAN, BIGFOOT, Engage Group B/E 123/23, 27k, Track South, Hostile, NW Quadrant.”
    • Press W to fire. The track must be inside a valid Patriot wedge or NASAM ring, or else the shot is considered trashed.
  4. VANISH
    Tracks vanish automatically 6 seconds after a valid engagement. You may also press K to manually remove a track early.

Track Identification

Use the TAGS button to cycle through display modes:

  • OFF – Clean scope
  • B/E – Bearing and distance from Bullseye
  • JTN – Track number labels
  • INFO – Heading, altitude, speed
  • ALL – Full info overlay
  • ALT – Altitude only

Rules of Engagement & Friendly Fire

  • Friendlies may appear at any time.
  • Firing on a friendly inside firing geometry: –20 pts (Fratricide).
  • Firing on a friendly outside geometry: –1 (Bird trashed) + –5 (ROE violation) = –6 pts.
  • SL should not issue COVER on friendly tracks — but errors are possible.

TBM & FIREBALL Events

  • TBMs appear in triangular waves from 200+ NM.
  • Click the FIREBALL button before impact to earn +2 points.
  • If TBMs impact without interception: –2 points.

Cards & Track Interactions

  • Left-click a unit to view its status card.
  • Right-click to pin the card in the top-right corner.
  • Cards display ID, heading, altitude, speed, and system status.

Scoring

  • +1 – Valid missile kill
  • +2 – FIREBALL intercept
  • –1 – Bird trashed (fired with no coverage)
  • –5 – ROE violation (invalid target)
  • –6 – Friendly fire outside geometry
  • –20 – Fratricide inside geometry
  • –2 – TBM impact

Controls & Hotkeys

  • C – COVER
  • A – AFFIRM
  • N – NEGATE
  • W – AWAY (engage)
  • K – KILL (manual vanish)
  • O – Aim selected radar wedge
  • R – Reset Bullseye position
  • Arrow Keys / + / – – Pan and zoom
