How to Play

In Practice Mode you run up to 10 Cover→Engage→Away→Kill sequences:

  1. Cover: Tap or click the prompted track and press A (Affirm). If you press N (Negate), it tags “NEGATE” and you remain in Cover until A.
  2. Engage: Tap the same track and press W (Away).
  3. Kill: Tap and press K. If you don’t within 10 s, it auto-kills.

You earn 1 point per completed sequence. After 10 rounds, you see GAME OVER with your score.

Hotkeys

  • C: Cover (free mode)
  • A: Affirm
  • N: Negate
  • W: Away
  • E: Engage
  • K: Kill
